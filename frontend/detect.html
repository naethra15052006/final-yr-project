Common Coconut Diseases

Bud Rot

Caused by Phytophthora palmivora. Affects growing bud, causing rot.

High

Leaf Blight

Caused by Bipolaris incurvata. Affects leaves with brown spots.

Medium

Stem Bleeding

Caused by Ceratocystis paradoxa. Stem exudates reddish gum.

High

Root Wilt

Caused by Phytoplasma. Roots rot and plant wilts.

Critical
